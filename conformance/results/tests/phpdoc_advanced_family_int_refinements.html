Integer sign refinements (negative-int, non-zero-int, …)

Group: Advanced PHPDoc types · Category: ecosystem · File: phpdoc_advanced_family_int_refinements.php

negative-int, non-positive-int, non-negative-int and non-zero-int are sign-bounded refinements of int. Analyzers that model them reject an out-of-range literal; others fall back to plain int and accept it.
